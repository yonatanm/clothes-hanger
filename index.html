Shop notes
  1. Stock: beech, maple, cherry or walnut. Run the grain along the width.
  2. Hook slot first: while the blank is still square, cut the slot for the flat hook (see template) at the top center.
  3. Front profile: glue the template onto the face and cut it on the bandsaw. Tape the offcuts back on.
  4. Top profile: lay the template on the top edge and cut again. This compound cut gives the thin waist and the wide shoulders.
  5. Shape: round everything over with a spokeshave, rasp and card scraper, then sand to 220–320.
  6. Finish: stain if wanted, then shellac or lacquer. Mask the shoulder tips.
  7. Tips: use a flocking kit (adhesive plus fibers) on the shoulders, or wrap them in thin suede.
  8. Hook: a flat chrome hanger hook (about 11 × 3.5 mm strip), epoxied into the slot.
